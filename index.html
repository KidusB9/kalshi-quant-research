Chart four

Can it forecast better than the price?

Every forecast this system makes is scored against what actually happened, next to the market price at the same moment. The measure is the Brier score, which is just the squared distance between the forecast and the truth. Lower is better. Beating the market price is the entire game, because the price is free and available to everyone.

So far the answer is no. On every desk measured, the market price is the better forecast. That result is published here at the same size as anything else. The live shadow book has scored 1,376 forecasts against settlement.

The graveyard

What was tested and killed

Most of the work here produced a negative result. These are the strategies that were built, measured, and switched off. They are listed because a record that only shows survivors is not a record.

World Cup Poisson totals pricer

Went 2 for 12 live and lost $18.60 on its only tournament. It sold fat-tailed deep unders and averaged more of them into a 10-0 runaway it had priced at 1 in 1,326. Retired July 19.

Political event harvesting

Backtested on 6,615 settled political markets across 953 price paths. Negative in every parameter cell, both halves of the sample, and every family. Removed July 15.

Pre-game favorite convergence

Looked profitable until the accounting was fixed. Once hidden stop-loss round trips were counted the true live record was about -$90 at a 67% win rate. Demoted to signal only on July 19 and still there.

Crypto rung sniping

The oracle half works: a free price read lands within $7.80 of the settlement print. The trade half does not exist. Across 9,063 observations the already-decided side asked exactly $1.00 in 100% of the final thirty seconds.

Daily temperature ladders

1,676 rungs that were already decided by the observation, over 68 days and 20 cities. The certain side was priced at exactly $1.00 in 100.0% of them. There was never a trade here.

Hourly weather ladders

A real edge, unreachable. Buying the decided side won 103 of 103 for +11.49 cents per contract, but the opportunity decays to zero in about four minutes and the fastest free feed publishes 123 to 154 seconds late. Signal only while the race is measured.

Language model forecasting

Scored against settlement on thousands of markets. The model loses to the market price by roughly double the Brier score. The chart above is the current standing.

How to verify this page

Anyone can claim a track record after the fact. The defense against that is not a promise, it is a timestamp you do not control.

  1. The commit history is the audit trail. Every version of this page is a separate commit, dated by GitHub, not by me. Rewriting a past number would mean rewriting history in public, and the previous version stays readable at the commit log.
  2. Check the data file, not just the prose. The same numbers are published next to this page as record.json. Pull two dated versions and diff them; the daily change should reconcile.
  3. The numbers come straight from my ledgers. Equity is the end-of-day mark recorded at the time. The settled records come from my risk system, which reads filled orders. Nothing here is reconstructed after the fact or recomputed from a nicer angle.
  4. This page posts every day, win or lose. Same time, fixed routine, no exceptions. A bad day cannot be held back, softened, or delayed into a good one; it prints next to the good ones and stays there.

Behind this page right now: 1,077 portfolio risk snapshots, 7 positions open at the last mark, 39 daily equity marks.

What is deliberately not here: entry rules, position sizing, risk limits, or anything else that would let a reader copy the system rather than check it. Results are public. The machinery is not.
